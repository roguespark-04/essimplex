Nody Bot

Battery Ethernet phone-home for datacenter construction

Hang it on the far end of a cable run. It sleeps for most of the build, wakes on a schedule, and POSTs a heartbeat so you know the drop is still alive — without PoE, without staying pingable, and without a mobile app.

How it works

Sleep. Wake. Prove it. Sleep.

Built for multi-month cable plant builds — not always-on monitoring. Website + device only; no mobile apps.

  1. 01

    Deep sleep

    Radio and MCU off. No listeners. Battery conserved for the long haul of the build schedule.

  2. 02

    Wake + DHCP

    Timer fires (~every 15 minutes). Ethernet comes up; the module obtains an address on the plant network.

  3. 03

    POST heartbeat

    JSON status — unit id, battery, MAC, and more — POSTed to your collector endpoint.

  4. 04

    Confirm & sleep

    Wait for HTTP 2xx (up to three tries), then return to deep sleep until the next wake.

Why Nody Bot

Proof of life when the plant isn’t finished

During AI / HPC hall construction, cable drops sit unfinished for months. You need to know they still work — without leaving something pingable on an incomplete network, and without PoE at the far end.

Who it’s for

  • Datacenter construction and cable verification crews
  • Commissioning teams that need proof of life across hundreds of drops
  • Integrators building AI / HPC halls on multi-month plant schedules

Product truth

  • Battery-powered Ethernet phone-home (outbound only)
  • Not ICMP-pingable — no always-on listener
  • Purpose-built for datacenter construction timelines
  • Hardware stack: ESP32 · W5500 · OLED · LiPo

Not pingable by design

Deep sleep between wakes. No ICMP, no open ports waiting for scans on an unfinished plant.

Months on battery

LiPo power — not PoE — so the far end works before power distribution is ready.

Your collector

Heartbeats POST to your endpoint. This site is marketing; collector / ops software ships separately.

Provisioning

USB + etctl

Configure units over USB with the etctl tool before they hit the plant. No mobile apps — website + device only.

  • Set collector URL, unit identity, and wake interval
  • Verify battery and Ethernet bring-up on the bench
  • Ship configured modules to the far-end install crew

What ships: Nody Bot module (ESP32 · W5500 · OLED · LiPo) and the USB provisioning path. Collector backend is separate.
