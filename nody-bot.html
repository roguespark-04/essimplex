How it works

Sleep. Wake. Prove it. Sleep.

Built for multi-month cable plant builds, not always-on monitoring. One cycle, repeated for the life of the build.

  1. Wake
  2. DHCP
  3. POST
  4. 2xx
  5. Sleep

  1. Step 1 of 5

    Wake

    A timer fires about every 15 minutes. Between wakes the radio and MCU are off, with no listeners, so the battery lasts the build schedule.

    ~15 min, configurable

  2. Step 2 of 5

    DHCP

    Wired Ethernet comes up on the W5500 and the module obtains an address on the plant network.

    W5500no Wi-Fi dependency

  3. Step 3 of 5

    POST

    A JSON heartbeat with unit id, battery, MAC and more is POSTed to /et/phone-home on your collector. The server is set as an IPv4 address; the device does no DNS lookup.

    HTTP POSTIPv4 server

  4. Step 4 of 5

    2xx

    The unit waits for an HTTP 2xx from the collector, with an 8-second timeout and up to three tries.

    8 s timeoutup to 3 tries

  5. Step 5 of 5

    Sleep

    It returns to deep sleep until the next wake. Nothing listens in between, so it is never pingable.

    deep sleep

Why Nody Bot

Proof it’s alive when the plant isn’t finished.

During AI and HPC hall construction, cable drops sit unfinished for months. You need to know they still work, without leaving something pingable on an incomplete network and without PoE at the far end.

  • Not pingable by design

    Deep sleep between wakes. No ICMP, and no open ports waiting for scans on an unfinished plant.

  • Months on battery

    LiPo power, not PoE, so the far end works before power distribution is ready.

  • Your collector

    Heartbeats POST to your endpoint. This site is marketing; collector and operations software ships separately.

Who it’s for

  • Datacenter construction and cable verification crews
  • Commissioning teams that need live confirmation across hundreds of drops
  • Integrators building AI and HPC halls on multi-month plant schedules

Provisioning

Configured over USB with etctl.

Units are set up on the bench before they reach the plant. The product surface is this website and the hardware device.

  1. Bench

    Set the collector address (IPv4), unit identity and wake interval

  2. Verify

    Verify battery and Ethernet bring-up on the bench

  3. Ship

    Ship configured modules to the far-end install crew

What ships

The Nody Bot module (ESP32, W5500, OLED, LiPo) and the USB provisioning path. The collector backend is separate.
