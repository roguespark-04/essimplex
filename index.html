Our first product

Nody Bot

Battery Ethernet phone-home for datacenter construction

Not dead yet.

A compact hardware module that hangs on the far end of a cable run and wakes on a schedule to prove the drop is still alive — through months of AI datacenter cabling, without PoE and without staying pingable.

  • Outbound HTTP heartbeat only — not ICMP-pingable
  • LiPo battery for multi-month build schedules
  • USB provisioning via etctl
