01Products

Nody Bot

Not dead yet.

Battery-powered Ethernet phone-home for datacenter construction. It hangs on the far end of a cable run and wakes on a schedule to prove the drop is still alive, through months of cabling, without PoE and without staying pingable.

Open the Nody Bot page
Reachability
Outbound HTTP heartbeat only.Not ICMP-pingable. No always-on listener on an unfinished network.
Power
LiPo battery for multi-month build schedules.Works at the far end before power distribution is ready.
Provisioning
Configured over USB with etctl.Set up on the bench, then shipped to the install crew.
