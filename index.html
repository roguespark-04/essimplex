Our first product

Nody Bot

Battery Ethernet phone-home for datacenter construction

Not dead yet.

A compact hardware module that hangs on the far end of a cable run and wakes on a schedule to prove the drop is still alive — through months of AI datacenter cabling, without PoE and without staying pingable.

  • Outbound HTTP heartbeat only — not ICMP-pingable
  • LiPo battery for multi-month build schedules
  • USB provisioning via etctl

About

Argumentum Vitae

Argumentum Vitae is a small hardware company focused on custom design and practical industrial products. Nody Bot is our first product, built for datacenter construction teams.

Our name is Latin for “proof of life” — the same job Nody Bot does for every cable drop it watches.

We keep claims honest: this marketing site describes the product surface (website + device). Collector / ops software ships separately. Branding here uses our company unicorn mark; Nody Bot is a product name, not the company.
